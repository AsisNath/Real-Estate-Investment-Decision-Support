## 1. Short-Term Rental (STR) Rules — ⚠ HIGH ATTENTION

- **Registration mandatory; platforms blocked otherwise.** Local Law 18 (2022) requires hosts to register with the Mayor's Office of Special Enforcement before listing any stay under 30 days, and prohibits Airbnb/VRBO/Booking.com from processing transactions for unregistered listings. — [NYC Rules — Registration and Requirements for Short-Term Rentals](https://rules.cityofnewyork.us/rule/registration-and-requirements-for-short-term-rentals/) — as of 2026-07-19 ✅ official
- **Host must be present, primary residence only, max 2 paying guests, no locked internal doors.** A non-owner-occupied investment unit cannot legally operate as an STR in NYC. — [OSE Registration Law page](https://www.nyc.gov/site/specialenforcement/registration-law/registration.page) — as of 2026-07-19 ✅ official page identified; details cross-checked via secondary guides (OSE page blocked automated fetch)
- **Fees:** $150 initial registration + $75 annual renewal, plus a $1.50/unit/night fee (effective March 2025). — [HostStarter guide](https://hoststarter.net/new-york-city-short-term-rental-license/) — as of 2026-07-19 ⚠ secondary; confirm with OSE
- **Enforcement is active and escalating:** since April 2025 OSE has issued Notices of Intent to Revoke to registered hosts found in violation (~20% of registered listings flagged by June 2025). — [Blank Rome LLP](https://www.blankrome.com/publications/metamorphosis-nycs-short-term-rental-laws) — as of 2026-07-19 ⚠ secondary (law-firm alert)
- **Buildings may also be on the city's Prohibited Buildings List** (owners can bar STRs building-wide). Whether 250 5th Ave is listed: **unverified — check the OSE portal directly.**

## 2. Landlord–Tenant Law (State of New York — HSTPA 2019)

- **Security deposit capped at one month's rent** statewide; no additional deposits/fees (no last-month prepay, pet, or key deposits). — [NYC Rent Guidelines Board — Security Deposits FAQ](https://rentguidelinesboard.cityofnewyork.us/resources/faqs/security-deposits/) — as of 2026-07-19 ✅ official page identified; cross-checked via secondary guides
- **Return deadline: 14 days** after move-out with itemized statement — miss it and the landlord **forfeits the entire deposit**, even for otherwise-valid deductions. — [NYS HCR Fact Sheet #9](https://hcr.ny.gov/fact-sheet-9) — as of 2026-07-19 ✅ official page identified; cross-checked
- **Buildings with 6+ units:** deposit must sit in an interest-bearing NY account, interest paid to tenant (landlord may keep 1% admin fee). — [Obligo guide](https://www.obligo.com/states/new-york-security-deposit-laws-a-complete-guide-for-landlords-tenants) — as of 2026-07-19 ⚠ secondary
- Late fees capped at the lesser of $50 or 5% of monthly rent (HSTPA). — [Landager — NY late fee laws](https://landager.com/en/property-compliance/usa/new-york/late-fees) — as of 2026-07-19 ⚠ secondary; confirm statute (RPL § 238-a)

## 3. Rent Regulation — ⚠ HIGH ATTENTION (three-layer regime)

- **Layer 1 — Rent Stabilization (~1M units):** if the building is stabilized (typically pre-1974, 6+ units), the Rent Guidelines Board sets increases. **On June 25, 2026 the RGB adopted a historic FREEZE: 0% for one-year AND two-year leases commencing Oct 1, 2026 – Sep 30, 2027.** — [RGB Adopted Guidelines 2026-27](https://rentguidelinesboard.cityofnewyork.us/adopted-summary-of-guidelines-2026-27/) — as of 2026-07-19 ✅ official (fetched and confirmed)
- **Layer 2 — Good Cause Eviction (market-rate units):** state law effective 2024-04-20; rent increases above the "local rent standard" (CPI + 5%, max 10%) are presumptively unreasonable, and lease renewal is effectively required absent good cause. Key exemptions: units rented out of a **condo or co-op**, buildings ≤10 units where the owner lives in-building, post-2009 construction, and high-rent units (>245% of FMR — currently ~$6,004 for a 1BR). — [NYS HCR — Good Cause Eviction](https://hcr.ny.gov/good-cause-eviction), [NYC HPD](https://www.nyc.gov/site/hpd/services-and-information/good-cause-eviction.page) — as of 2026-07-19 ✅ official pages identified; thresholds cross-checked
- **Layer 3 — exempt units:** condo/co-op rentals and other exempt categories face no statutory increase cap.
- **Investor implication:** which layer applies is decided by **build year, unit count, and unit type** — a stabilized unit at this address rents at 0% growth through Sep 2027; a market-rate rental may still be Good-Cause-capped near CPI+5%; a condo unit rented out is exempt from both. **Confirm the unit's DHCR registration history before underwriting any rent-growth assumption.**

## 4. Condo / Co-op Restrictions (NYC's HOA equivalent) — ⚠ VERIFY PER-PROPERTY

- **Co-op:** boards commonly require 1–2 years' owner occupancy before subletting, limit sublets (e.g., 2 of every 5 years), charge sublet fees (~20–25% surcharges reported in Park Slope), and hold **full discretion** to deny tenants. A co-op unit is generally a poor pure-rental vehicle. — [Brick Underground](https://www.brickunderground.com/guides/how-to-buy/sublet-restrictions-in-a-co-op-vs-condo), [DeFalco Realty](https://www.defalcorealty.com/blog/coop-sublet-policy-nyc/) — as of 2026-07-19 ⚠ secondary
- **Condo:** rental policies are far more liberal — usually only a right of first refusal and a minimum lease term (often 6 months; stays <30 days are illegal under LL18 regardless). — [Brick Underground](https://www.brickunderground.com/guides/how-to-buy/sublet-restrictions-in-a-co-op-vs-condo) — as of 2026-07-19 ⚠ secondary
