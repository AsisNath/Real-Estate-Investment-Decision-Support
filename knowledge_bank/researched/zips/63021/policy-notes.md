## 1. Short-Term Rental (STR) Rules — ⚠ HIGH ATTENTION — DEAL-BREAKING FOR A NON-OWNER-OCCUPIED INVESTMENT

- **Short-term rental is available only to a "Permanent Resident," and a Permanent Resident must actually live there.** The ordinance defines a Permanent Resident as an owner *or lessee* who maintains the dwelling as a **primary residence**, documented by at least two of: motor vehicle registration, driver's license, voter registration, bank statement, or utility bill. — [City of Ballwin, Bill No. 3991 § 15.A](https://www.ballwin.mo.us/pageimages/Board/2018/BILL_3991_Short_Term_Rentals.pdf) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official (ordinance text read directly)
- **The resident must occupy the property at least 180 days in any one-year period.** Verbatim: "The Permanent Resident shall reside on the property at least 180 days within a one-year period." **A purely tenanted investment property cannot satisfy this.** — [Bill No. 3991 § 15.B.2](https://www.ballwin.mo.us/pageimages/Board/2018/BILL_3991_Short_Term_Rentals.pdf) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **Rentals must be within the principal residential structure** — "not within a detached building or structure." A guest house, coach house, or converted garage cannot be rented short-term even by a qualifying resident. — [Bill No. 3991 § 15.B.1](https://www.ballwin.mo.us/pageimages/Board/2018/BILL_3991_Short_Term_Rentals.pdf) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **The definition of short-term rental carries no minimum-night threshold.** It is simply "the leasing of a dwelling, or portion thereof, to overnight guests." This is broader than the 30-day test used in most Missouri cities — on its face it reaches *any* overnight paid stay, and renting a single room counts. — [Bill No. 3991 § 15.A](https://www.ballwin.mo.us/pageimages/Board/2018/BILL_3991_Short_Term_Rentals.pdf) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **Permit and inspection required before offering, $100.00 permit fee, renewed every two years.** The permit is issued under the same procedures as occupancy permits. — [Bill No. 3991 § 15.B.3](https://www.ballwin.mo.us/pageimages/Board/2018/BILL_3991_Short_Term_Rentals.pdf) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **A 24/7 local contact must respond to complaints within 45 minutes.** The name and telephone number of both the Permanent Resident and the local contact must be filed with the City in writing before the permit issues. This is a far tighter standard than Maryland Heights' one-hour-drive rule. — [Bill No. 3991 § 15.B.3](https://www.ballwin.mo.us/pageimages/Board/2018/BILL_3991_Short_Term_Rentals.pdf) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **Two citations within 180 days of each other put the business license and STR permit up for revocation**, and a revoked licensee is barred from holding an STR permit for **one year**. — [Bill No. 3991 § 15.C.2](https://www.ballwin.mo.us/pageimages/Board/2018/BILL_3991_Short_Term_Rentals.pdf) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **Also required:** all vehicles of the resident and guests parked on the property or the abutting street; records of every stay kept **two years** and available for inspection (dates leased, dates of stay, guest name and contact); maximum occupancy per the Code; and compliance with property-maintenance, nuisance, zoning, and building codes. A business license is separately required. — [Bill No. 3991 § 15.B](https://www.ballwin.mo.us/pageimages/Board/2018/BILL_3991_Short_Term_Rentals.pdf) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **Unverified:** whether the codified text at Appendix A, Art. XVI, § 30.15 has been amended since Bill 3991 was adopted in 2018. Municode blocked automated access. **Confirm the current text with Ballwin Code Enforcement, 1 Government Center, (636) 227-2129, inspections@ballwin.mo.us, before relying on any figure above.** — as of 2026-08-02 ⚠ official source unreachable

## 2. Landlord–Tenant Law (State of Missouri)

- **Security deposit capped at two months' rent.** Verbatim: "A landlord may not demand or receive a security deposit in excess of two months' rent." — [RSMo 535.300, Missouri Revisor of Statutes](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=535.300) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **Return deadline: 30 days after the tenancy ends**, either the full deposit or a written itemized list of damages withheld plus the balance. — [RSMo 535.300](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=535.300) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **Wrongful withholding costs double:** "the tenant shall recover as damages twice the amount wrongfully withheld." — [RSMo 535.300](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=535.300) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **Long-term leasing is unaffected by the STR ordinance.** Bill 3991 regulates overnight-guest rentals only; a conventional 12-month tenancy is not a Short Term Residential Rental and does not require the STR permit. Ballwin does, however, require an **occupancy inspection on change of occupancy**, and the owner is responsible for correcting violations found. — [City of Ballwin, Building Permits & Occupancy Inspection](https://www.ballwin.mo.us/Building-Permits-and-Occupancy-Inspection-Information/) — as of 2026-08-02 ⚠ secondary; confirm scope and fee with Code Enforcement

## 3. Rent Control — INFO (positive for the investor)

- **Missouri preempts local rent control statewide.** Verbatim: "No county or city...may enact, maintain, or enforce any ordinance or resolution which regulates the amount of rent to be charged for privately-owned, single-family, or multiple-unit residential or commercial rental property." Neither Ballwin nor St. Louis County can cap rent or limit increases. — [RSMo 441.043](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=441.043) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **Three carve-outs, none applying to a market-rate purchase:** property owned by the county or city, voluntary agreements with private landlords over subsidized rentals, and property receiving community development block grant funding. — [RSMo 441.043](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=441.043) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **Investor implication:** rent growth is constrained by the market, not by law, and this is a *statutory* protection — a future Board of Aldermen cannot introduce a cap without the General Assembly acting first.

## 4. HOA / Deed Restrictions — ⚠ VERIFY PER-PROPERTY

- **Missouri has no HOA-specific statute; an association's power comes from its recorded declaration plus the Missouri Nonprofit Corporation Law.** There is no statutory ceiling on how far a Missouri HOA may restrict leasing, which makes the recorded CC&Rs or subdivision indenture the controlling document. — [Missouri HOA Laws, Homeowners Protection Bureau](https://www.hopb.co/missouri) — as of 2026-08-02 ⚠ secondary
- **Unverified for this specific property:** whether 745 Woodrun Dr is subject to a subdivision indenture, trustees, or HOA, and whether it restricts leasing. West St. Louis County subdivisions of this era commonly have **trustee indentures** that function like an HOA without the name — the same structure found in the Charlestowne indenture already in this knowledge bank. **Obtain the recorded indenture and all amendments from the St. Louis County Recorder of Deeds, and ask the trustees in writing whether any leasing restriction exists.** A private restriction can bar long-term leasing even though the city permits it.

## 5. Lodging Tax — applies only if an STR is lawfully operated

- **St. Louis County lodging tax is reported at 7.25% total** — 3.75% Convention and Tourism Tax plus 3.5% Convention and Sports Tax — filed quarterly within 20 days after each calendar quarter, and applying to transient home rentals booked through Airbnb, VRBO, and similar platforms. Moot unless the owner-occupancy test is met. — [St. Louis County STR guide, BNBCalc](https://www.bnbcalc.com/blog/short-term-rental-regulation/St-Louis-County-Missouri-Guide) — as of 2026-08-02 ⚠ secondary; **confirm rates and filing duty with the St. Louis County Department of Revenue before modeling STR income**
