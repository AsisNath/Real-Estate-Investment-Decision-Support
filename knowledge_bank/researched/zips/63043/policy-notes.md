## 1. Short-Term Rental (STR) Rules — ⚠ HIGH ATTENTION

- **A permit is required, and STR means any stay under 30 consecutive days.** Maryland Heights adopted STR regulations effective **January 1, 2024**, administered by the Department of Community Development. — [City of Maryland Heights, Short-Term Rental](https://www.marylandheights.com/departments/community_development/building___codes/short-term_rental.php) — as of 2026-08-01 ✅ official
- **Fees: $75 initial application, plus a $25 annual inspection fee.** Inspections are both initial and annual, so underwrite the $25 as a recurring yearly cost, not one-time. — [City of Maryland Heights, Short-Term Rental](https://www.marylandheights.com/departments/community_development/building___codes/short-term_rental.php) — as of 2026-08-01 ✅ official
- **No owner-occupancy requirement — this is the decisive finding for a non-owner-occupied investment.** The rule is that "the owner **or manager** must live within a one-hour drive to ensure responsiveness." A property manager satisfies it, so an out-of-area investor can operate an STR here provided they retain local management. Many Missouri jurisdictions do impose primary-residence conditions; Maryland Heights does not. — [City of Maryland Heights, Understanding Short-Term Rental Regulations](https://www.marylandheights.com/news_detail_T31_R2099.php) — as of 2026-08-01 ✅ official
- **Whole-dwelling rentals only.** Renting individual rooms is prohibited; the stated purpose is preventing multi-family occupancy of single-family homes. Room-by-room revenue models are not available here. — [City of Maryland Heights, Understanding Short-Term Rental Regulations](https://www.marylandheights.com/news_detail_T31_R2099.php) — as of 2026-08-01 ✅ official
- **Occupancy caps, rental-duration limits, and parking control all exist but the specific numbers are unverified.** The city states the ordinance limits the number of occupants, limits the duration of the rental, and specifies required parking control, but publishes the figures only in the application PDF, which returned HTTP 404. **Get the current numbers from Community Development at (314) 738-2232 or planning@marylandheights.com before modeling nightly capacity.** — as of 2026-08-01 ⚠ unverified; official source unreachable
- **Signage is prohibited and the City Council can revoke a permit for noncompliance.** Permit revocation is a real operating risk, not a theoretical one — a revoked permit ends STR income entirely while the mortgage continues. — [City of Maryland Heights, Understanding Short-Term Rental Regulations](https://www.marylandheights.com/news_detail_T31_R2099.php) — as of 2026-08-01 ✅ official
- **Lodging tax applies, but the rate is disputed across sources.** The city's own announcement describes a "0.5 cent lodging tax"; third-party STR guides describe a Maryland Heights transient guest tax of 5%–7.5%. These are not reconcilable, and several of those guides appear to conflate the *state of Maryland* with *Maryland Heights, Missouri*. **Do not model a tax rate from either figure — confirm the current rate and base with the city's finance department.** — as of 2026-08-01 ⚠ secondary sources conflict with the official page

## 2. Landlord–Tenant Law (State of Missouri)

- **Security deposit is capped at two months' rent.** Statutory text: "A landlord may not demand or receive a security deposit in excess of two months' rent." Unlike Indiana and several neighbors, Missouri does set a ceiling. — [RSMo 535.300, Missouri Revisor of Statutes](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=535.300) — as of 2026-08-01 ✅ official
- **Return deadline: 30 days after the tenancy ends.** Within that window the landlord must either return the full deposit or furnish a written itemized list of damages withheld along with the balance. — [RSMo 535.300](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=535.300) — as of 2026-08-01 ✅ official
- **Wrongful withholding costs double.** "If the landlord wrongfully withholds all or any portion of the security deposit in violation of this section, the tenant shall recover as damages twice the amount wrongfully withheld." A missed deadline is a direct cash loss, not a paperwork slip. — [RSMo 535.300](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=535.300) — as of 2026-08-01 ✅ official
- **Deposits must be held in a federally insured bank, credit union, or depository institution,** and the tenant must get reasonable written notice of the move-out inspection date and time. — as of 2026-08-01 ⚠ secondary; confirm the full text of RSMo 535.300 with counsel

## 3. Rent Control — INFO (positive for the investor)

- **Missouri preempts local rent control statewide.** Statutory text: "No county or city...may enact, maintain, or enforce any ordinance or resolution which regulates the amount of rent to be charged for privately-owned, single-family, or multiple-unit residential or commercial rental property." Neither Maryland Heights nor St. Louis County can cap your rent or limit increases. — [RSMo 441.043, Missouri Revisor of Statutes](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=441.043) — as of 2026-08-01 ✅ official
- **Three carve-outs, none of which apply to a market-rate purchase:** property owned by the county or city, voluntary agreements with private landlords over subsidized rentals, and property receiving community development block grant funding. Decisive only if the property carries an affordability covenant or CDBG money — check for one. — [RSMo 441.043](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=441.043) — as of 2026-08-01 ✅ official
- **Investor implication:** rent growth here is constrained by the market, not by law. Note this is a *statutory* protection, not merely an absence of local ordinance — a future city council cannot introduce a cap without the General Assembly acting first.

## 4. HOA / Deed Restrictions — ⚠ VERIFY PER-PROPERTY

- **Missouri has no HOA-specific statute; an association's power comes from its recorded declaration plus the Missouri Nonprofit Corporation Law.** There is no state statutory ceiling on how far a Missouri HOA may restrict leasing, which makes the recorded CC&Rs the controlling document. — [Missouri HOA Laws, Homeowners Protection Bureau](https://www.hopb.co/missouri) — as of 2026-08-01 ⚠ secondary
- **HOAs can and do prohibit or condition short-term rentals** through Airbnb or VRBO where their governing documents authorize it. A restriction exceeding the authority granted by the CC&Rs or the Nonprofit Corporation Act can be struck down, but that is litigation, not underwriting. — [Missouri HOA Laws (2026), iPropertyManagement](https://ipropertymanagement.com/laws/missouri-hoa-rules-regulations) — as of 2026-08-01 ⚠ secondary
- **Unverified for this specific property:** whether this parcel is subject to an HOA, subdivision trustees, or recorded deed restrictions, and whether any of them restrict leasing or STR use. Maryland Heights contains many subdivision-trustee arrangements that function like HOAs without the name. **Obtain the recorded declaration, indentures, bylaws, and all amendments from St. Louis County Recorder of Deeds, and ask the association in writing whether any rental or STR restriction exists.** A private CC&R ban overrides the city's permission entirely — the city permitting STR does not mean your subdivision does.
