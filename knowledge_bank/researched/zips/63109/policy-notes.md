## 2. Landlord-Tenant Law (State of Missouri)

- **Security deposit cap:** Missouri caps residential security deposits at 2 months’ rent. — [RSMo § 535.300](https://www.revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?bid=28387&section=535.300) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **Security deposit return deadline:** Within 30 days after tenancy termination, the landlord must return the deposit or provide an itemized damage list plus any balance. — [RSMo § 535.300](https://www.revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?bid=28387&section=535.300) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **Move-out inspection notice:** The landlord must give reasonable written notice of the inspection date/time, and the tenant has the right to be present. — [RSMo § 535.300](https://www.revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?bid=28387&section=535.300) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **Month-to-month termination:** Missouri requires one month’s written notice to terminate a month-to-month tenancy. — [RSMo § 441.060](https://www.revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=441.060) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **Nonpayment eviction process:** For rent-and-possession cases, Missouri requires rent to be due, demanded, and unpaid before filing; the one-month notice under § 441.060 is not required for that chapter’s relief. — [RSMo § 535.020](https://www.revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?bid=28367&section=535.020) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **Local rental compliance:** St. Louis has a mandatory registration process for residential rental units through the Housing Conservation Program, with inspections/certificates referenced in Ordinance 71835. — [Ordinance 71835](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/government/city-laws/ordinances/ordinance.cfm?ord=71835) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official

## 3. Rent Control

- **Statewide preemption:** Missouri bars counties and cities, including charter cities, from enacting, maintaining, or enforcing ordinances regulating rent amounts for privately owned residential or commercial rental property, with exceptions for government-owned/subsidized/CDBG-assisted properties. — [RSMo § 441.043](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?bid=24343&section=441.043) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
- **Investor impact:** No private-market rent-growth cap was identified for this St. Louis residential rental because state law preempts local rent regulation. — [RSMo § 441.043](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?bid=24343&section=441.043) — as of 2026-08-02 ✅ official
