## 1. Short-Term Rental (STR) Rules — ⚠ HIGH ATTENTION

- **STR permit required; city effectively closed to new residential STRs.** Ordinance #22-104 (effective 2022-08-02) requires an annual Short-Term Rental Permit plus, for residentially zoned property, a Conditional Use Permit requiring a public hearing and City Council vote. — [City of St. Charles — Short Term Rentals](https://www.stcharlescitymo.gov/1259/Short-Term-Rentals) — as of 2026-07-26 ✅ official
- **Residential STR cap effectively full.** A 2024–2025 moratorium (Ordinance #24-085) was lifted by an amendment on 2025-04-15 that lowered the citywide cap from 0.5% to **0.15% of total housing units (~48 units)** and restricted *new* residential-zone STR permits to the Extended Historic Preservation (EHP) district only. The city is at capacity and is **not currently accepting new residential STR applications** outside the EHP district. — [City of St. Charles — Short Term Rentals](https://www.stcharlescitymo.gov/1259/Short-Term-Rentals) — as of 2026-07-26 ✅ official
- **Charlestowne is very unlikely to be in the EHP district** (EHP covers the historic downtown/Frenchtown riverfront area; Charlestowne is a 2006 suburban subdivision) — **unverified, confirm the parcel's zoning district with St. Charles Community Development (636-949-3222)** before assuming STR is unavailable outright. — [City of St. Charles Short Term Rentals page](https://www.stcharlescitymo.gov/1259/Short-Term-Rentals) — as of 2026-07-26 ⚠ secondary (inference, not a direct zoning-map confirmation)
- **500-foot buffer rule.** A residential STR must be at least 500 feet from any other existing/approved residential STR — largely moot given the cap above, but relevant if the cap is ever raised. — [City of St. Charles — Short Term Rentals](https://www.stcharlescitymo.gov/1259/Short-Term-Rentals) — as of 2026-07-26 ✅ official
- **Bottom line for underwriting:** do not model STR income for this property. New residential permits are not being issued, and Charlestowne does not appear to qualify for the one exception (EHP district) even if a slot opened up.

## 2. Landlord–Tenant Law (State of Missouri)

- **Security deposit capped at two months' rent.** — [RSMo § 535.300](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=535.300) — as of 2026-07-26 ✅ official
- **Return deadline: 30 days after tenancy ends**, either returning the full deposit or an itemized statement of deductions with any balance. — [RSMo § 535.300](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=535.300) — as of 2026-07-26 ✅ official
- **Penalty for wrongful withholding: double damages** (2x the wrongfully withheld amount) if the landlord misses the deadline or fails to itemize. — [RSMo § 535.300](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=535.300) — as of 2026-07-26 ✅ official
- **Landlord must offer a move-out inspection**, with written notice of date/time and the tenant's right to be present. — [RSMo § 535.300](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=535.300) — as of 2026-07-26 ✅ official
- No state-mandated interest on deposits; no state-mandated return method (check or electronic transfer both acceptable). — [ipropertymanagement.com summary](https://ipropertymanagement.com/laws/missouri-security-deposit-returns) — as of 2026-07-26 ⚠ secondary; cross-checked against the official statute above for the load-bearing figures

## 3. Rent Control — INFO (positive for the investor)

- **Missouri bans rent control statewide.** No county or city may enact or enforce any ordinance regulating rent for private residential or commercial rental property. — [RSMo § 441.043(1)](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=441.043) — as of 2026-07-26 ✅ official
- **New statewide preemption (2025) further limits local landlord-tenant regulation.** House Bill 595 (signed 2025-07-14, effective 2025-08-28) bars cities/counties from requiring landlords to accept a tenant's source of income (e.g., housing vouchers), from restricting use of credit/background checks, from capping security deposits below state law, or from imposing a tenant right-of-first-refusal on sale. — [Missouri Senate — HB 595 Bill Information](https://www.senate.mo.gov/BillTracking/Bills/BillInformation?year=2025&billid=4959328) — as of 2026-07-26 ✅ official
- **Investor implication:** rent growth is capped only by the market, not by law, and the city/county cannot add tenant-favorable screening or deposit rules beyond current state law.
