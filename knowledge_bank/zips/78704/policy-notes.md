## 1. Short-Term Rental (STR) Rules — ⚠ HIGH ATTENTION

- **License required.** Any rental of less than 30 consecutive days requires a City of Austin STR license through Development Services. Three license types: Type 1 (owner-occupied), Type 2 (non-owner-occupied whole home), Type 3 (multi-family). — [City of Austin — Short-Term Rentals](https://www.austintexas.gov/development-services/short-term-rentals) — as of 2026-07-19 ✅ official
- **Fees:** New license $836.30 ($789 license + $47.30 notification); renewal $385.30. Fees non-refundable. — [City of Austin](https://www.austintexas.gov/development-services/short-term-rentals) — as of 2026-07-19 ✅ official
- **License term:** Two years (changed from one year in October 2025). *Note: several third-party guides still describe licensing as "annual" — the official city page supersedes them.* — [City of Austin](https://www.austintexas.gov/development-services/short-term-rentals) — as of 2026-07-19 ✅ official
- **⚠ Active enforcement as of July 1, 2026:** the City now requests removal of unlicensed properties from Airbnb/VRBO, and platforms must display the license number in listings. An unlicensed STR strategy is no longer viable in Austin. — [City of Austin](https://www.austintexas.gov/development-services/short-term-rentals) — as of 2026-07-19 ✅ official
- **Penalties:** $500–$2,000 per violation, per day. — [Avalara MyLodgeTax](https://www.avalara.com/mylodgetax/en/blog/2025/10/austin-short-term-rental-hosts-marketplaces-face-new-regulations.html) — as of 2026-07-19 ⚠ secondary source; confirm with city
- **Tax obligation:** Quarterly Hotel Occupancy Tax (HOT) reports required. — [City of Austin](https://www.austintexas.gov/development-services/short-term-rentals) — as of 2026-07-19 ✅ official
- **Processing time:** 6–10 weeks for new licenses — factor into any STR-dependent underwriting timeline.

## 2. Landlord–Tenant Law (State of Texas)

- **Security deposit return:** within 30 days of tenant surrender (Tex. Prop. Code § 92.103); clock starts when tenant vacates AND provides a written forwarding address. — [Texas State Law Library](https://guides.sll.texas.gov/landlord-tenant-law/security-deposits) — as of 2026-07-19 ✅ official
- **No cap on deposit amount** under state law.
- **Itemized deduction list required** if withholding any portion (§ 92.104). — [Texas State Law Library](https://guides.sll.texas.gov/landlord-tenant-law/security-deposit-refunds) — as of 2026-07-19 ✅ official
- **Bad-faith penalty:** $100 + 3× amount wrongfully withheld + attorney fees (§ 92.109). — [Texas State Law Library](https://guides.sll.texas.gov/landlord-tenant-law/security-deposits) — as of 2026-07-19 ✅ official
- Lease may require 30–60 days' written move-out notice as a condition of deposit return (lease-dependent, not statutory).

## 3. Rent Control

- **None, and none possible.** Texas has banned municipal/county rent control since 1993 (Tex. Gov't Code ch. 2143 preemption); only exception is a governor-approved disaster declaration. **Investor implication: no regulatory ceiling on rent growth assumptions, but model market-driven limits instead.** — [Texas State Law Library — Rent](https://guides.sll.texas.gov/landlord-tenant-law/rent) — as of 2026-07-19 ✅ official

## 4. HOA / Deed Restrictions — ⚠ VERIFY PER-PROPERTY

- Texas HOAs **may adopt and enforce leasing restrictions, including STR bans**, via dedicatory instruments (Tex. Prop. Code § 209.016(d)); CC&R amendments typically require a supermajority owner vote. — [Texas Property Code ch. 209](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/docs/PR/htm/PR.209.htm) — as of 2026-07-19 ✅ official
- HOAs **cannot** require tenant approval or demand tenant credit reports; they may collect basic occupant info and lease dates (§ 209.016). — [statutes.capitol.texas.gov](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/docs/PR/htm/PR.209.htm) — as of 2026-07-19 ✅ official
