How notes change a report

A note is read automatically whenever a matching property is analyzed. If it ends with a | Flag | Severity | Why | table, those rows appear in Policy Restrictions and Sources, and every HIGH row becomes a risk that can change the recommendation.

A note can also state machine-readable limits that are checked against the investor's own assumptions. Add a section titled NorthStar Machine-Readable Summary with lines like rent_growth_cap_percent: 3 or short_term_rental_allowed: false.
